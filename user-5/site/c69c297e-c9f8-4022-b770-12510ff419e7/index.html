About Technova

TECHNOVA 2025 is the flagship annual symposium hosted by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering. It's a vibrant platform designed to ignite innovation, foster collaboration, and challenge the brightest minds.

Over two action-packed days, students engage in cutting-edge technical challenges and thrilling non-technical showdowns, all curated by industry experts and faculty mentors.

From hackathons and coding sprints to creative arts and gaming arenas — TECHNOVA offers something for every passionate learner.

General Rules

Guidelines every participant must follow

Eligibility

  • Open to all UG & PG students
  • Valid college ID card mandatory
  • Registration confirmation required

Registration

  • Online pre-registration compulsory
  • On-spot registration limited
  • One student can join multiple events

Conduct

  • Maintain discipline at all times
  • Judges' decision is final
  • Malpractice leads to disqualification

Team Events

  • Team size as per event guidelines
  • Cross-college teams allowed
  • Substitutions not permitted mid-event

Certificates

  • Participation e-certificates for all
  • Merit certificates for winners
  • Distributed post-event via email

Reporting

  • Report 30 mins before event
  • Latecomers may be disqualified
  • Carry own laptops where specified
